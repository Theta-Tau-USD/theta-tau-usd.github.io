Why Rush Theta Tau

Theta Tau is the place where engineering students find the people who make the major worth it. You get upperclassmen who have taken your classes, brothers who share notes and study late with you, and a network that keeps going long after graduation.

Rushing gets you access to all three of our pillars at once: a brotherhood that shows up for you, service projects that put your skills to work, and professional events with the companies our members already intern and work at.

What Is Rush

Rush is our recruitment period at the start of each semester. It is a week of open events where you meet the chapter, see how we work, and decide whether Theta Tau is right for you. Every event is open to any engineering student, and there is no cost to attend.

Fill out the interest form to get event reminders and updates. When you are ready to join, submit the application and we will follow up about an interview and next steps.
