Why Rush Theta Tau?

At USD, Theta Tau is a community of engineering students who strive to grow both in the classroom and beyond it. We connect you with likeminded peers while opening the doors through mentorship, leadership opportunities, and events made to help you advance your career.

Whether you're looking to build your resume or find a group of people to go through the major with, Theta Tau has a place for you.

What Is Rush?

Rush is our recruitment period, held at the start of each semester over three days of open events. It's your chance to meet the chapter and see how we operate, and decide if Theta Tau is the right fit for you. Every event is open to any engineering student at USD, free of charge.

Fill out the interest form for reminders and updates, submit application and we'll follow up with next steps.
